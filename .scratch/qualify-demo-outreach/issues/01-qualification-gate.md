## Question

What gates a lead into "build them a demo"? Manual judgment vs. scored criteria, and where does it sit relative to the existing Kanban stages (Novo → Contatado → Respondeu → Fechado)?

## Answer

Manual judgment gate, not scored criteria, for v1. "Worth building a demo" depends on things that are hard to score cheaply right now (does the business look real/active, does the category fit what's crediby buildable, gut read on willingness to pay). At solo/low volume this isn't a bottleneck, and there's no reply-rate data yet to validate a scoring formula against.

Mechanically: a flag/action per lead (e.g. a "Qualificar" toggle) that unlocks the demo-generation action, not necessarily a new Kanban column. Exact UI mechanic left to implementation (see map's "Not yet specified").
