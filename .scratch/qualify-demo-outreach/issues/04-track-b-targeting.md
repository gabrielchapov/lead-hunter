## Question

Does Track B (automation-prototype pitch) target the same lead pool as Track A (any no-website local business), or does it need different targeting criteria?

## Answer

Different targeting, not the same pool. A solo salon owner or single mechanic shop doesn't have operationally complex enough day-to-day work to justify custom automation, and likely can't afford it — but is still exactly the right target for Track A (a free website demo costs them nothing to receive).

Track B only makes sense for businesses complex enough to be genuinely drowning in a spreadsheet: chains, car dealerships, real estate agencies, insurance brokers, equipment dealers — this lines up closely with Caliper's actual case studies (auto dealership, agricultural machinery, insurance; see map Notes).

This surfaced a real gap: current sourcing (OSM/Overpass, "local business near X, category Y") is built for Track A's pool, not Track B's ("bigger/multi-location/more complex operations" isn't a category OSM models). Deferred as its own research question — see ticket 08.
